Money Notebook

A personal Android app that keeps a private notebook of where money goes, built from bank messages, bank emails and statements.

What it does

  • Notes payments by itself. It reads bank and card alerts from text messages and, if you connect Gmail, from bank emails.
  • Reads statements. From bank and credit-card statement PDFs it picks up the summary: statement date, balance, amount due, due date and credit limit.
  • Keeps a khata. A simple ledger of money lent to and received from friends and family.
  • Shows the big picture. Spending, income and balances month by month, with amounts hidden until you tap to see them.

Private by design

  • Your records stay on your phone and your own computer. Money Notebook has no servers.
  • Gmail access is read-only, and the app looks only at emails from banks and card companies.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of data.
